# Film still assets — Juna Aditya

Eight original photographs were generated with the built-in `image_gen` tool for this fictional director portfolio. The subjects, identity, film titles, and synopses are demonstration material. Every photograph is a single image without text, logos, or watermarks.

The template uses the eight relative WebP asset paths documented below. The 24-second silent montage at `assets/rangkaian-frame.mp4` was assembled from these same stills; it is a demonstration video, not footage from a produced film.

`thumbnail.webp` is a 1200 × 750 WebP encoding of the approved desktop portfolio screenshot. The self-hosted Inter 700 font and its SIL Open Font License are documented in `assets/fonts/sources.json` and `assets/fonts/OFL.txt`. Helvetica is used when available on the visitor's device; Inter is the bundled fallback.
